"Vinta: compra/venta" "Vinta: buy/sell"

Diseño de prototipo de una aplicación para compra/venta de ropa usada. Prototype design for an app for buying and selling second hand clothes.

Se diseño un prototipo en Figma con el objetivo de crear una aplicación para el celular que busca ofrecer un lugar rápido y seguro para la compra-venta de ropa usada. Se trabajo con insights que continuaron con diagramas de flujo, diversos wireframes y testeos que permitieron llegar al prototipo final. The prototype was designed in Figma with the aim of creating a mobile app designed to provide a quick and secure platform for buying and selling second-hand clothing. The process involved analysing user insights, creating flowcharts, developing various wireframes and conducting user tests, all of which helped to arrive at the final prototype.
